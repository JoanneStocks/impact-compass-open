# Impact Compass

Impact Compass is an experimental AI-assisted research prototype exploring how retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), semantic search, and researcher profiling can help connect academic expertise with UK Parliamentary inquiries.

## The Problem

Researchers often discover parliamentary inquiries and evidence-gathering opportunities too late to contribute effectively.

Impact Compass explores whether AI systems can help identify relevant parliamentary activity based on a researcher's publications and areas of expertise.

## Current Status

Impact Compass is an active research prototype and remains under development.

Formal evaluation has not yet been completed.
