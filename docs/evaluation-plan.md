# Evaluation Plan

## Objective

The primary objective is to determine whether Impact Compass can identify Parliamentary inquiries that are genuinely relevant to a researcher's expertise.

## Approaches to Compare

The planned evaluation will compare:

1. Keyword matching
2. Theme-based matching
3. Semantic retrieval

## Initial Manual Evaluation

An initial pilot evaluation is planned using a sample of researchers.

Proposed process:

- Select 10–20 researchers
- Generate the top 5 inquiry recommendations for each researcher
- Review each recommendation manually
- Record relevance assessments

## Relevance Categories

Recommendations will be assessed as:

- Relevant
- Partially Relevant
- Not Relevant

## Success Measures

Potential measures include:

- Precision at Top 5
- Percentage of relevant recommendations
- Comparison between matching approaches
- Consistency of explanations

## Future Evaluation

Future work may include:

- User testing with researchers
- Comparative retrieval studies
- Evaluation across disciplines
- Longitudinal assessment of recommendation quality

The goal is to move from anecdotal testing towards a more systematic assessment of performance.
