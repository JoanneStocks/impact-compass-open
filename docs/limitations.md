# Current Limitations

Impact Compass is an experimental research prototype and should be understood within that context.

## No Formal Evaluation Yet

Development decisions have been informed by iterative testing and manual review of generated matches.

A formal evaluation study has not yet been completed.

## Researcher Identity Challenges

Accurately identifying a researcher's publication record remains difficult.

Although ORCID improves attribution, effectiveness depends on profile completeness and maintenance by individual researchers.

## Inquiry Matching Limitations

Parliamentary inquiries represent only one form of policy engagement.

A strong inquiry match should not be interpreted as evidence of policy impact or future policy influence.

## LLM Limitations

Large language models are used for several analytical tasks within the workflow.

These systems may:

- Over-generalise
- Miss important contextual information
- Generate imperfect explanations
- Produce inconsistent outputs

Human judgement remains important when interpreting results.

## Coverage Limitations

The current prototype focuses primarily on UK Parliamentary inquiries.

Many other routes to policy influence are outside the current scope of the project.

## Prototype Status

Impact Compass is intended as a research and experimentation platform rather than a production-ready system.
